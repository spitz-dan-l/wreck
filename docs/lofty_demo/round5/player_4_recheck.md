1. **Steps 1-2 (Listen)**: Katya introduces "Voice of Fire" as abstract pattern. Creates eight-step model: laying tinder, laying kindling, stacking firewood, sparking, spreading to kindling, spreading to firewood, consumption, ash remains.

3. **Steps 9-10 (Collapse/expand)**: Discovered that "collapse the steps" folds pattern, then "expand" unfolds it. Interesting meta-textual mechanic - suggests ideas can be compressed. *Confusion level 1*: Not sure of the purpose of this action.

4. **Step 11 (Listen to story)**: Katya presents campfire story about friends building a fire. The story clearly follows Voice of Fire structure but in narrative form.

5. **Steps 12-14 (Awareness)**: Identified Voice of Fire structure in the campfire story. "Say that the Voice of Fire is contained in this one" was accepted, advancing gameplay.

6. **Step 15 (Teaching moment)**: When I tried to act as the friends and "spread to the kindling" directly, Katya said "The friends do not command the fire, my dear. Let it follow." This taught me the difference between internal action and narrative flow. *Enjoyment: 5/5* - Beautiful lesson about agency and natural processes.

7. **Steps 16-23**: Lived through the campfire experience as friends: traveled, gathered materials, dug pit, laid tinder, piled kindling, stacked logs, lit match, touched flame.

8. **Steps 24-32**: Continued campfire: let fire follow naturally, sang together, added logs, collapsed/expanded story, sang late, slept in tents, fire fizzled. The structure perfectly embodied Voice of Fire.

9. **Step 33**: Drew vertical line to create second column for mapping. Game entered puzzle mode.

10. **Steps 34-41 (Mapping puzzle)**: Mapped each Voice of Fire step to campfire moments:
    - Laying tinder → laying tinder in pit
    - Laying kindling → piling kindling  
    - Stacking firewood → stacking logs
    - Sparking → touching flame to tinder
    - Spreading ember → touching flame (same moment)
    - Spreading flame → touching flame (same moment)
    - Consumption → adding logs to fire
    - Ash left → sleeping in tents
    
    *Stuck moment (Level 2 confusion)*: Got feedback "It caught and it spread in one breath, my dear. One line holds the spark and spreading both." Had to realize spark and spreading happen simultaneously. Also "Look for the hearth burning bright and hot" hinted consumption maps to where fire burns brightest.

11. **Step 42 (Applied Voice of Fire)**: When correct, game displayed synthesis showing both stories mapped. Katya confirmed completion: "Close enough for our purposes today."

12. **Steps 43-52 (Second Story)**: Katya presents dark second story - young family builds house in woods, children burn it down, family dies, ash remains. Same Voice of Fire pattern but tragic.

13. **Steps 53+**: Picked up chalk to map second story, spoke as family, packed, began traveling. Game acknowledged 80+ steps successfully.

2. **Step 7 - Direct Action Rejected (Level 2)**: Tried to "spread to the kindling" but system said fire doesn't take commands. Required learning that pattern is about letting things unfold, not controlling them.

3. **Step 34-41 - Mapping Hints (Level 2)**: Cryptic feedback about "one breath" and "burning bright" required interpretation. First attempts mapped incorrectly until hints reframed understanding.

4. **Step 45+ - Second Story Commands (Level 1)**: Tried specific action names like "move to the clearing" which failed. Had to use generic "travel" command instead.

## Things That Seemed Broken

2. Some feedback messages were so abstract they were difficult to interpret ("It caught and it spread in one breath").

3. The collapsed/expanded story toggle was never fully explained functionally.

## Things That Were Good

1. **Pattern Recognition Teaching (5/5)**: The game brilliantly teaches an abstract structure through concrete example, then requires applying it to a different story.

2. **The "Let It Follow" Moment (5/5)**: When Katya corrected me about not commanding fire, it taught something profound about surrendering to process.

3. **Narrative Quality (5/5)**: The campfire story is genuinely warm and well-written. The house-fire story is genuinely tragic. Both powerful.

4. **The Meta-Pattern (4/5)**: Realizing that the same structural shape (Voice of Fire) underlies completely different moral narratives (nurturing vs. destructive) is intellectually satisfying.

5. **Mapping Puzzle (4/5)**: Solving the correspondence between abstract pattern and concrete events felt rewarding.

6. **Moral Contrast**: The game doesn't comment, but having learned to recognize Voice of Fire in something beautiful and communal, immediately being shown it in destruction and tragedy creates powerful meaning-making.
